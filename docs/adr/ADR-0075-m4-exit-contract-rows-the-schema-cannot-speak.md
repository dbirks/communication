# ADR-0075 — Two § 7 exit rows the accepted schema and seed cannot speak: C4c's `finished_at IS NULL` and W9's "carol (project B only)"

- **Date:** 2026-11-05
- **Status:** Accepted (contract amendment; the measured arms are the m4 exit driver's C4c and W9 rows — see Verification)
- **Owners:** m4 exit contract + lab maintainers
- **Related decisions / contracts:** `docs/m4-contract.md` § 7.1 C4c, § 7.2 row W9, § 7.1 C9d, § 7.1 C7c, § 7.1 C10b; ADR-0016 § 3 (the lease lies: a terminal row without `finished_at` is one of them), ADR-0023 § 5.4 / ADR-0074 (the reconciler's re-claim writes `interrupted` + `finished_at` in one statement), ADR-0012 (mock-IdP lab identities); schema `migrations/00005_m4_pipelines.sql` (`attempts_terminal_timestamped`, `attempts_queued_unowned`); `docs/compatibility/probes/m4/seed.sh` steps 2 and 2b. Lab evidence: acceptance run 41983100425 (commit d82abbe) — the `fence` shard's `GAP C4c finished_at IS NULL (§ 7.1's literal expectation)` row and the `core-b` shard's two `GAP W9 …` rows.

## Context

The m4 exit gate's rule is that a row is only ever PASS (measured), FAIL (measured and wrong) or GAP (the lab produced nothing to measure) — a GAP is an unproven claim that still lets the job pass, so the m4 exit pass means zero GAPs. Two § 7 rows can never be measured as literally written, and a third was double-booked against the seed:

* **§ 7.1 C4c** asserts `select status, outputs is null, finished_at from attempts where id='$ATT'` answers `interrupted | t | NULL`. Postgres refuses that row: migration 00005's `attempts_terminal_timestamped` CHECK states `(status IN ('succeeded','failed','cancelled','interrupted')) = (finished_at IS NOT NULL)` — the third of ADR-0016 § 3's three lease lies, made structural. A fenced attempt is terminal, and a terminal row **without** `finished_at` is unrepresentable; the store's own re-claim (ADR-0023 § 5.4, ADR-0074) writes `interrupted` together with `finished_at = now()`. Observed: `finished_at='2026-11-05 06:58:58.32704+00'`. The row as written asks for a lie; the claim C4 actually owns — *a stale owner cannot publish success* — is entirely about what the dead attempt **published**: nothing.
* **§ 7.2 W9** names its observer "carol (project B only)", but § 7.1 C9d races carol against dan as **maintainers of the M4 project** on one CAS decision. One identity cannot hold both roles, so `seed.sh` step 2b provisions the project-B-only role as a dedicated second IdP identity — **erin**, created as the only seeded member of `m4-project-b` and holding no membership row on the M4 project — and step 2 keeps carol where an executable arm needs her. The contract's literal name therefore describes a person the seed must not make an outsider, and W9's observer row could only ever GAP.
* Adjacent (recorded here because the same exit pass measures them): **§ 7.1 C7c's** eval-time arm is reachable, because the publish-time reference table only refuses a selection the declared output schema *closes* — a schema that names the field as an optional property (or admits unknown keys) publishes, and the same read faults in evaluation. **§ 7.1 C10b's** retry arm is reachable through a gate-authored reviewer step and a wider retry backoff; the shipped `m4-exit.sh` (generated by `make_fixture_repo.sh`, which the lab must not edit) answers byte-identically on every attempt, so the driver authors its own revision instead.

## Decision

1. **§ 7.1 C4c is amended** (the text is in `docs/m4-contract.md`, § 7.1 C4c): the assertion is that the fenced attempt's own row reads `interrupted | t | t` for `status, outputs is null, finished_at is not null`, and that the dead attempt published nothing — zero ready artifacts, zero `run_checkpoints`, zero `node.attempt.completed` rows for it — with the replacement attempt's strictly greater fencing token as the proof the refusal is mechanical. The `finished_at` half is stated as what the schema makes true: a terminal row MUST carry its terminal timestamp, and that timestamp is the reconciler's write (ADR-0016 § 3, ADR-0074), not the fenced worker's.
2. **§ 7.2 row W9 is amended** to name its observer as the dedicated seeded project-B-only identity — `erin`, `seed.sh` step 2b, member of `manifest.project_b` and of no other project — with the reason written into the row: § 7.1 C9d books carol as an M4 maintainer, so the two roles cannot share one identity. Every W9 arm (the four deep links, the DOM leak search, the SSE request log, the console) is graded on that seeded identity's own session, and the observer arm is a measured verdict over the membership rows the ledger actually holds.
3. **The lab instruments these rows are measured with** (recorded, and § 7.1 C7c/C10b's rows are
   clarified in place to say so): **C7c's eval-time arm** is reachable and now measured, because the
   publish-time reference table (`internal/pipelines/expr/compile.go`'s `checkNodeRef`) refuses a
   selection only where the declared output schema *closes* it — a revision whose review node declares
   `verdict` as an optional property (declared, not `required`) publishes fine while the shipped review
   step never produces it, so the predicate faults in evaluation and the SCOPE fails at once with
   `predicate_error`; the closed-schema document remains the validate-time arm. **C10b's retry arm**
   runs a gate-authored reviewer revision whose reply is malformed on the first attempt of the chain and
   valid on the next, with a widened `retry.backoff` so the until scope can be *sampled* while the
   rejected reply is being retried; its "no route was taken" half is graded on the non-terminal
   node-execution vocabulary taken from 00005's own status CHECK (`pending|ready|leased|running|waiting`
   — the until scope sits at `ready` while its body's attempt is in backoff), which the pre-amendment
   waiting-set omitted and which is why a real mid-failure sample was thrown away as "already closed".
   Concretely, the C10b arm's step is `sh -c` over a POSIX script that counts its own attempts in a
   marker file (`m4-review-attempts.count`) inside the node's `config.cwd` — the run workspace's
   `scratch`, the one directory a reviewer with `workspace.mode: read_only` repositories may write
   (`internal/workspace/git/plan.go`'s `RoleScratch` is granted rw unconditionally) and the reason the
   counter survives a retry: a retry is a `continue_workspace` recovery
   (`internal/workspace/git/plan.go`'s `continueRun`), so the file the first attempt wrote is still
   there for the next, and a fresh run gets a fresh workspace. `retry.maxAttempts` stays at the seed's
   **2** — the row counts attempts against that budget, and raising it would move the thing under
   measurement — only `retry.backoff.baseSeconds` is widened (30 s), so the scope row can be read
   *inside* the failure window. `executable: sh` is resolved by the worker's own `exec.LookPath`
   (`internal/worker/procexec.go`'s `resolveExecutable`) and nono grants the resolved binary's directory
   per launch (`internal/sandbox/nono/launchplan.go`'s `ToolReads`), i.e. the authored step runs under
   the same sandbox rules as the shipped ones; a new file in `/opt/m4/bin` was rejected precisely
   because that directory is `rm -rf`'d and regenerated by `make_fixture_repo.sh` on every stack bring-
   up, so anything the driver installed there would vanish under a later worker's re-generation.
   **W9's `/approvals/{id}` arm** reads a real approval of the M4 project out of `approval_requests`
   (C11/C14 publish approvals on that project inside the same `core-b` shard, and § 7.2 orders C before
   W); only when the ledger holds none does the leg park one by posting a twin run and leaving it on its
   `/approve` gate, which it cancels again in its own `finally` — the leg's single mutation, and the
   outsider never receives the id. The observer's membership facts are read with W9's own reader rather
   than `c9_role_of`, because that helper answers "psql failed" and "no membership" with the same empty
   string, and an absence is exactly the fact the row rests on.
   Neither instrument touches the shipped fixture helper (`make_fixture_repo.sh` is not edited), the
   schema, the API surface or the security boundary.

## Consequences

* Both § 7 rows are now falsifiable: a fenced attempt that closes without a terminal timestamp, or an outsider who turns out to hold an M4 membership row, is a FAIL with the observed values printed, not a GAP.
* The exit driver's C4c leg no longer prints a GAP line that would let a genuinely broken fence read as "unmeasurable"; it prints one measured row per half of the amended claim.
* W9's observer arm names the person it measures in every label, so the report cannot be misread as carol's session. Nothing about C9d changed.
* Limitation: the design-handoff brief (`communication-ui-brief`, `communication-spec/10`) still says "as carol" in prose; those files are read-only authority for a *different* agent and are deliberately untouched here. § 7.2's row is the executable statement of the arm and now says who plays it.
* The C10b routing row can now be sampled on the authored arm when the malformed twin's shipped-speed window closes too fast for any read, and the row says **which** run supplied the sample (`sampled on ADR-0075's authored arm`). It is never graded twice: the malformed twin's rows still carry C10a (the refusal, the JSON-Schema location, the silence) and the authored arm carries the retry's success, so one claim has exactly one row.
* Limitation: the C10b retry arm is measured on a gate-authored revision, not on the seed twin (whose reviewer answers byte-identically every attempt); the seed twin's all-malformed run still carries the "no route was taken" arms, which is where the silent-coercion bug would have shown up.

## Security/privacy

No security boundary moves. C4c's amendment removes a demand that would have required *weakening a persistence invariant* (a terminal attempt row with no terminal timestamp is precisely the lease lie ADR-0016 § 3 makes unrepresentable), so the change is a strict improvement in what the store can prove about a stale owner. W9's amendment keeps the outsider an outsider: the arm is only graded when the ledger confirms she holds no membership on the M4 project and is not an org admin (an org admin can see every project through `internal/authz` `CanView` and can never stand in for the row's outsider). The deep-linked approval row is real M4 data, and what W9 asserts is that it renders nothing to her — 404 from the control plane, a not-found state in the DOM, no project name, no node names, no digest values, and no opened event stream. No secret, token or credential appears in the contract, the ADR, the driver's authored documents, or the fixture helper's replies.

## Verification

## Migration/rollback

Docs + lab only — no migration, no API, no protocol change, nothing to roll back in a deployment. To revert, restore the two § 7 rows' previous text and the C4c/W9 legs' verdict calls in `e2e/m4/e2e_m4_exit.py`; the pre-amendment behaviour is exactly the GAP lines quoted in Context, which is what this ADR removes. Forward-only by construction: the C4c amendment can never re-open the persistence invariant, because the invariant lives in the CHECK the amendment defers to.

## Addendum 2026-11-05 (pass 42066375840) — § 7.1 C1j's agent leg on launcher pi: the "agent asked" premise is a launcher property, and pi never asks

**Measured FAIL** (Actions run 42066375840, commit 2bb9fed, `m4-core-a-1/m4.out:152`):
`FAIL C1j agent: at least one conversation.permission.requested — 0 request(s), 0 decision(s)`, while
C1c/C1d/C1g/C1h/C1i all PASS and the other three C1j invariants PASS. The agent arm completed — for
the first time — on launcher **pi** (`pi-acp` on `david-birks/qwen3-8-flash-next` via
`compose.m4-llm.yml`), after ADR-0069 finally gave the never-asks launcher a mode it may run under.

**Root cause — observed, not guessed, in four links:**

1. **The seeded mode is not a prompting mode.** `.github/workflows/acceptance.yml` sets
   `COMMUNICATION_LAB_LAUNCHER=pi` (no Claude token on runners); `seed.sh` maps that launcher to
   `LAB_MODE=autonomous_within_sandbox` (its own comment: the three prompting modes "stay
   fail-closed on it and the only mode it may run under is autonomous_within_sandbox — which
   claims no review"). The run's m4-implementer conversation row confirms launcher pi (m4.out:854).
2. **The worker wiring is correct.** `internal/worker/agentturn.go` + `turnloop.go` put
   ask/deny/preapproved into the launcher's verified prompting mode and **fail closed** on pi
   (observed live: attempt `1389e01a-7e44-51ba-ae62-4ca9eb5644ae`, "no verified prompting mode …
   would be a lie about review", docs/compatibility/ACP.md pi row); the autonomous arm keeps
   `modeID=""` *deliberately* — "the adapter's never-asks default IS what this mode says it runs".
   The launcher card records the fact: `AskModeID: ""` for pi (`internal/agent/acp/launchers.go`).
3. **pi has no prompting path for tool execution.** The pinned npm artifact was re-read for this
   addendum: registry `pi-acp` `dist-tags.latest = 0.0.34` (same version the worker image pins,
   `worker/Dockerfile` `PI_ACP_VERSION`), and in `package/dist/index.js` of `pi-acp-0.0.34.tgz` the
   **only** `conn.requestPermission` (i.e. `session/request_permission`) call site is
   `requestExtensionPermission` (:1365), reached solely from the `extension_ui_request` handler
   (:1231 → `handleExtensionInput`/`handleExtensionConfirm`) — pi *extension* UI dialogs. The
   `tool_execution_start|update|end` cases (:1097/:1163/:1181) only **report** tool calls as
   `session/update` notifications; none gates them. pi's advertised `modes` are its thinking
   levels, not permission modes. There is no setting the launcher could write that changes this;
   this matches the 2026-10-24 lab inspection recorded in docs/compatibility/ACP.md and
   SANDBOX.md row P4, and `internal/agent/acp/acp-observed.json` holds **no** `permission_prompts`
   row for pi (a cell with no row is not_tested by definition, ADR-0051).
4. **The row did PASS — on a launcher that asks.** ADR-0036's addendum records pass 29 (claude):
   `live29/C1.out:97-:100`, 8 requested / 8 decided, every one
   `decided_by=node_policy:preapproved_within_sandbox`. "The agent asked" was always a claim about
   the launcher's prompting mode, and the m4 lab's launcher choice changed underneath it.

**Options considered:**

| Option | Why rejected / chosen |
|---|---|
| (a) Worker misconfiguration — write a flag so pi prompts | rejected: nothing is mis-set. The fail-closed refusal on pi is the product *working* (ADR-0019 D6); pi's adapter has no code path that prompts for tool calls, so no flag exists to write. |
| (b) Seed pi into a prompting mode | rejected: there is no such mode to seed. The only prompting round-trip is the extension-UI handler; shipping a lab-only pi extension that opens a confirm dialog would manufacture a "permission request" the *agent* never raised — a PASS-by-construction dressed as "the prompting-mode agent asked before it touched the workspace", exactly what ADR-0036's honest-verdict rule forbids. |
| (c) Amend the row to grade the claim the launcher can actually make (chosen) | the autonomous mode's own sentence — "runs without asking; the sandbox is the boundary" — is fully observable in the journal, and the "every ask closes with a named verdict" half stays proven on a deterministic prompting source. |
| Permanent GAP | rejected for the reason this ADR was written for: a GAP exits green while claiming nothing. |

**Decision.** § 7.1 C1j's agent leg becomes launcher-conditional (`docs/m4-contract.md` C1j;
`e2e/m4/e2e_m4_exit.py` `c1_permission_leg_verdicts`):

* Prompting launchers (`claude`, `codex`) and **any unknown launcher** (fail-closed default) run
  the original four arms unchanged — `permission_verdicts_ok` is byte-identical to the
  pre-amendment leg, and `test_c1_logic.py::C1PermissionLegLauncherTest` pins label-for-label
  equality so the other three checks cannot be weakened silently.
* Launcher `pi` (the only member of `C1J_AUTONOMOUS_LAUNCHERS`; a launcher joins it
  deliberately, never by omission) replaces the impossible `>=1 requested` premise with three
  **positive** measurements — no absence reads as a pass anywhere:
  (a) every agent attempt's profile row, read through `attempts.conversation_id →
  conversations.agent_profile_id → agent_profiles`, is exactly
  `pi/autonomous_within_sandbox/required` — an empty reading or any other mode/launcher/enforcement
  row FAILs (a `preapproved` row launching on pi would mean the fail-closed gate was bypassed);
  (b) `sandbox.verified` rows ≥ succeeded `agent.acp` attempts, with ≥1 succeeded agent attempt —
  the boundary the mode's sentence points at, ADR-0019 D6 fail-closed makes a shortfall a
  contradiction; (c) the run's deterministic prompting source — the `/approve` gate, which asks
  and receives a NAMED human verdict every run with no launcher and no model in the loop — shows
  each `approval.requested` with exactly one `approval.decided(decision=approved, decided_by set)`:
  the journal demonstrably closes the asks that really happen, so zero permission rows on pi is
  the launcher's silence, not a swallowed bridge.
* The three pair invariants (exactly-one-decided, status=decided +
  `decided_by=node_policy:preapproved_within_sandbox`, no orphan verdicts) are graded on **any**
  permission rows the journal holds, under every launcher. If pi ever gains prompting, rows are
  measured against ADR-0036 (a) — and a row decided by anything but the node policy then FAILs,
  forcing the ADR-0069 mode to be revisited in the open rather than silently satisfied.

**Consequences.** C1j is falsifiable on both launchers: on pi it now FAILs on a lying profile row,
an unverified launch, or an unclosed gate ask — all measured, none assumed; on claude nothing
moved. The amendment adds no event type, no payload field, no schema change and no seed change
(`seed.sh` already seeds the honest mode). Limitation stated plainly: **no live `agent.acp`
launcher on the acceptance runners currently produces `conversation.permission.*` rows**; the
positive evidence that a policy prompt journals `requested`+`decided` with the node-policy decider
remains the claude pass-29 rows (ADR-0036 addendum), and re-acquiring it on a prompting launcher
is a lab-credential decision, not something this row may fake.

**Verification.** Offline (`m4-gate-logic` job): `e2e/m4/test_c1_logic.py` — the prompting arm's
label-for-label equality (zero-request still FAILs on claude/codex/unknown), the six-arm pi leg
(empty/lying/mixed profile readings FAIL, sv<attempts FAILs, 0/0 FAILs, missing/unsigned/doubled
gate pair FAILs, orphan/cancelled/human-decided permission rows still FAIL under pi, garbage
inputs do not crash), and the parenthesisation discipline for the three new reads. Live: the
`core-a` shard's C1j pi arms on the next Actions m4 pass — **pending there, and not claimed until
the shard quotes them** (a mocked green never substitutes, `12`-test-strategy).
