 Why this matters
 Four on/off settings make 16 possible sidebars. Every sidebar change you just approved (01 renaming, 02 the split, 08 the two buttons, 09 removing ⋯) has to be built and checked in each layout it touches. Cutting three settings leaves 2 layouts instead of 16.
 Keep collapse, cut the rest RECOMMENDED . The tree is the default, and Ask placement becomes moot with finding 08. Folder filtering moves to the Workflows page, where there's room for it.
 Keep them all, but test them as product. Each preference becomes a documented mode with its own visual acceptance pass. This is expensive.
 Promote them to a first-class layout schema on the server (one ADR) so they stop riding on expanded . This fixes the storage but not the surface area.
